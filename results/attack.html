So reuse is refused twice

The chain accepts leaf i of epoch e only with nonce key LAMP + (e << 16) + i at sequence 0, which can happen once. The signer reads that state and keeps a journal:

  • signed payment 1 with leaf 0
  • chain: nonce key of leaf 0 now at sequence 1 (stub)
  • refused: leaf 0 is already used or pending; refusing to sign
  • signed payment 2 with leaf 1
